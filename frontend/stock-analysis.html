Chart symbols & data-quality notes

Red circles mark candles for which a supported detector returned an anomaly. Dashed blue and teal lines are the latest SMA and EMA values, not historical series. Dotted grey lines are Fibonacci levels derived from the returned range. Omitted bars are listed above instead of being interpolated.

Charting: TradingView Lightweight Charts v4.2.3 (Apache-2.0), vendored locally in vendor/.

Computed observations

Trend, momentum & price structure

Server-computed

SMA, EMA and Fibonacci lines appear only when the backend returns a usable value. Volume and volatility checks sit alongside the trend indicators because they describe a different part of the market's behaviour.

How to read these cards

“Available” means the backend returned a usable value for the selected window. "Unavailable" is deliberate: it usually means there were not enough bars with usable OHLCV, and no value has been substituted. Method notes describe the calculation without promising a trading outcome.

Potential anomalies

Not proof of manipulation

Red circles mark only candles for which a supported detector returned an anomaly. Historical markers are clustered per candle; latest-only detectors are labelled and are not drawn back through history. A flag is an unusual observation, not proof of manipulation and not a trading signal.

These indicators describe past price and volume behaviour. They are not predictions and not investment advice. Unavailable values are shown as “Unavailable”, never as made-up numbers. A statistical anomaly is not proof of manipulation; detectors can flag unusual observations without any manipulation occurring.

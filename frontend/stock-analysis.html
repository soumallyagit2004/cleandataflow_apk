Indicator overlays & derived indicators

SMA, EMA and Fibonacci price lines are shown only when the backend returns a usable value. Those lines are latest values, not historical series.

Statistical anomaly detections

Red circles mark only candles for which a supported detector returned an anomaly. LOF, volume spike, volatility anomaly and volume–price divergence are evaluated causally on historical bars. Autoencoder and Transformer remain latest-complete-bar checks and are never drawn as historical markers. Divergence is a signal without a numeric threshold; no score is invented for it. A flagged observation is not proof of manipulation and not a trading signal.

These indicators describe past price and volume behaviour. They are not predictions and not investment advice. Unavailable values are shown as “Unavailable”, never as made-up numbers. Statistical anomaly detectors can flag unusual observations without any market manipulation occurring.

Indicator overlays & derived indicators

These values are computed from the returned bars. SMA, EMA and Fibonacci price lines are shown only when the backend returns a usable value; the SMA/EMA lines are latest values, not historical series.

Statistical anomaly detections

LOF, Autoencoder and Transformer score only the latest complete returned bar. A flagged marker is a statistical outlier, not proof of manipulation and not a trading signal. Historical anomaly timelines are not shown because the backend does not return per-bar model scores.

These indicators describe past price and volume behaviour. They are not predictions and not investment advice. Unavailable values are shown as “Unavailable”, never as made-up numbers. Statistical anomaly detectors can flag unusual observations without any market manipulation occurring.

About Clean Data Flow

Ten ideas, in plain language

A short orientation for first-time visitors. No jargon wall — just what the platform does, what it cannot do, and how to read a result responsibly.

01

What this platform is

Clean Data Flow turns raw NSE market data and provider-supplied price history into readable charts, indicators and anomaly checks.

02

Why analyse the data

A price alone hides context. Volume, range and trend help you see whether a move was broad, quiet, sudden or unusual.

03

What an anomaly means

An anomaly is an observation that differs from its own recent history. It is a prompt to look closer — not proof of manipulation.

04

Technical indicators

SMA, EMA, RSI and Fibonacci levels summarise trend and momentum using only the bars the provider actually returned.

05

AI-assisted detection

LOF, autoencoder and transformer models compare the latest bar with historical patterns. They can flag unusual behaviour without explaining its cause.

06

Volume and price together

A price move on unusually high volume, or price and volume moving in opposite directions, can reveal changing participation.

07

How to search

Type a well-known company name or an exact symbol, choose NSE or BSE, pick a period and interval, then request real bars.

08

How to read results

Start with the source and freshness, then the chart, indicators and anomaly notes. If a value is unavailable, it stays unavailable.

09

Why freshness matters

Daily bars are delayed history, not a live tick feed. The latest bar can still be forming during market hours, and the fetch time and bar time tell you exactly how current the data is.

10

No return guarantees

Past patterns do not guarantee future returns. Nothing here is investment advice, and an anomaly is never proof of wrongdoing.

Transparency

Data sources & methodology

Indices & constituents

NSE India

Latest-session index levels and constituent prices through the backend. Not guaranteed live or tick-by-tick, and BSE-only indices such as Sensex are not available from this NSE endpoint.

Price history

Yahoo Finance

OHLCV bars fetched by the backend, marked as delayed and interval-dependent. Configured fallback providers are reported in the response source chain; no provider key is exposed to the browser.

Market headlines

Economic Times RSS

Publicly published market headlines with publisher links. Headlines, publication times and links pass through unchanged; retrieval time is recorded separately.

Backend connection

FastAPI health check

The frontend calls http://localhost:8000. Test the connection here; no data is invented if it is offline.
